For suppliersPrototype

Find the guidance you need to sell to the Australian Government

Supplier Navigation Assistant searches the Selling to Government and AusTender guidance and shows you the government's own words, with the page they came from. With AI on, Claude also explains those words in plain English and suggests what to ask next.

Free with a Claude account (a free one works). Search works as soon as you open it. Set up your own copy once, in about a minute, to turn on the AI explanations.

Set it up once

Claude only runs the AI explanations in your own copy, so make one the first time.

  1. Open the assistant.
    Open the assistant
  2. Sign in to Claude if asked. If you don't have an account, create a free one.
  3. Click the assistant's name at the top left of the page, then choose Duplicate. Your own copy opens.
  4. Try it in your copy. Press What is a panel? and choose Allow when Claude asks. An answer appears with a plain-English summary and the exact wording below it.

Next time, open your copy from your Claude artifacts. To bring in a colleague, send them this page rather than your copy's link, which only works for you.

No Duplicate option?

You can still use the original page. Open it and ask questions: search and the government's exact wording work without Claude. Only the plain-English summary, the next-step statements and the suggested questions need your own copy.

Each question

  1. Ask in your own words, or press one of the examples. You can leave Explain with AI on or switch it off.
  2. Read the exact wording. Every quote is in a blue-edged box with its source, link, last-updated date and the date it was retrieved. AI-written text is in a dashed purple box and marked as such.
  3. Follow on. The page lists what the sources point to next, related pages, who to contact, and questions you could ask next. Each suggested question can be answered from the published guidance.

What it does and does not do

  • FindsSearches 83 pages from sellingtogov.finance.gov.au, tenders.gov.au and help.tenders.gov.au, indexed 1 October 2026.
  • QuotesShows the government's exact wording, never a paraphrase presented as the source.
  • ExplainsWith AI on, restates the wording in plain English and says what the sources describe as coming next.
  • Says when it doesn't knowIf the pages don't cover a question, it says so and points to where to look.
  • Doesn't adviseIt won't say whether to bid, how to price, whether you qualify, or what a rule means for your business.
  • Doesn't pick tendersIt doesn't search, rank or recommend individual tenders. AusTender does that.

Questions

Does it cost anything?

No. It runs on your own Claude account. With Explain with AI on, each question is one Claude request that counts towards your plan's usage. Switch it off to search the sources without using Claude.

What happens to my question?

Your question and the matching passages from the published pages are sent to Claude under your own account. Nothing is sent anywhere else. Don't type confidential or personal information into the question box.

Can I rely on the answers?

Treat it as a finding aid. Quoted wording is copied from the published pages, so you can check it against the page it links to. Guidance changes, so check the original page and its date before you rely on it. The match strength shows how closely your words match a source. It is not a judgement that the page answers your question. The AI summary is written by Claude from the quoted passages only, and each next-step statement links to the page that supports it.

Is this an official government service?

No. It is a prototype that points you to Selling to Government and AusTender and does not replace them. Quoted government text is © Commonwealth of Australia.

Who made this?

It is a prototype in the same set as Requirement Quality Review, part of the Better Procurement project and built on the open-source Wayfinder Procurement Evaluation project.
