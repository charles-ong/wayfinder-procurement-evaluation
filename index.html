Better Procurement prototypes

Working prototypes from the Better Procurement project, exploring how to make Australian Government procurement easier to find, understand and act on, for both buyers and suppliers. Each one is a page you open in Claude, copy once, and then use on your own account.

Free with a Claude account (a free one works). You set a prototype up once in about a minute.

Requirement Quality Review

Reads your RFQ, RFT or Statement of Requirements, scores every requirement, and flags the ones likely to confuse suppliers or shut out small businesses.

  • Clarity, consistency and duplication
  • Proportionality to value and risk
  • Access for SMEs and new entrants, and undefined terms

Supplier Navigation Assistant

Helps small businesses, First Nations businesses and first-time suppliers find and understand what Selling to Government and AusTender already publish. It shows the government's exact wording and gives information, not advice.

  • Searches 91 published pages and quotes them with their source and date
  • With AI on, explains the wording in plain English and suggests what to ask next
  • Where to start, the procurement lifecycle, a glossary and where opportunities are listed

How these work

Claude only runs a prototype in a copy you own, so make one the first time.

  1. Choose a prototype below and press its set-up button. Each has a short page that says what to do and what to expect.
  2. Open the prototype and sign in to Claude if asked. A free account works.
  3. Click the prototype's name at the top left of the page, then choose Duplicate. Your own copy opens.
  4. Use your copy. Next time, open it from your Claude artifacts. To bring in a colleague, send them this page rather than your copy's link, which only works for you.
